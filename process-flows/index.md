# Process & Flows

## Employee Off-boarding Process

### Overview
The human resource department aims to facilitate a smooth off-boarding process for both the organization and employees. Departing employees should leave with positive experiences, memories, and mutual feelings of respect and appreciation. HR maintains good relations with departing employees as they act as brand ambassadors after their departure.

### Off-boarding Steps

1. **Initial Process**
   * Formal Resignation / Termination
   * Communication / Intimating resignation to concerned parties
   * One-on-one conversation with team lead/HR/HOD
     * Step 1 Exit interview to understand prime reason
     * Explore retention possibilities for high potential and star performers
   * Accepting Resignation
   * Determine Notice period
     * Note: If payroll occurs during notice period, salary processing will be adjusted accordingly

2. **Team Communication**
   * Notify Management and Teams
   * Notice period tasks management (with Supervisor involvement)
   * Detail all ongoing/pending tasks and their progress
   * Document direct communications and independent projects

3. **Knowledge Transfer**
   * Identify Replacement needs
     * Internal delegation
     * Split of duties
     * External resource hiring
   * Train Replacement or team member taking initial charge
   * Prepare off-boarding checklist/Charge handover

4. **Asset and Access Management**
   * Review and transfer:
     * Access/Logins
     * Key files and data
     * Important information and client records
     * Company Assets
     * Insurance

5. **Final Processing**
   * Address financial matters
   * Prepare farewell arrangements
   * Revoke system access and recover company assets
   * Process final settlement
   * Prepare exit documents:
     * Experience certificate
     * Recommendation letter
     * Tax challan
     * Outstanding employee reimbursements
