# Cellular Package

SOFT PYRAMID provides cellular packages to eligible employees to support their communication needs. Here are the key details of our cellular package policy:

## Eligibility
* All permanent employees who have completed their probation period
* Team leads and managers
* Client-facing roles requiring regular communication
* On-call support team members

## Package Details
* Monthly cellular allowance based on role and requirements
* Data package for internet connectivity
* SMS bundle for business communication
* Call minutes for work-related calls

## Package Tiers
Role | Monthly Allowance | Data | Minutes | SMS
---|---|---|---|---
Team Member | 1,000 PKR | 20GB | 1000 | 1000
Team Lead | 2,000 PKR | 40GB | 2000 | 2000
Manager | 3,000 PKR | 60GB | 3000 | 3000

## Terms and Conditions
* The package is for official use only
* Unused balance will not be carried forward
* Package can be revised based on business needs
* Company reserves the right to modify or withdraw the package

## How to Apply
* Submit request through HR portal
* Provide necessary documentation
* Package will be activated within 5 working days
* Monthly reimbursement through salary
