# Career Development

At SOFT PYRAMID, we are committed to helping our employees grow professionally and achieve their career goals. We offer various opportunities and resources for career development:

## Training and Development
* Regular internal training sessions and workshops
* Access to online learning platforms and resources
* Mentorship programs with senior team members
* Technical and soft skills development opportunities

## Professional Certifications
* Support for relevant professional certifications
* Reimbursement for certification exam fees (subject to approval)
* Study materials and preparation resources
* Time allocation for exam preparation

## Career Growth Path
* Clear career progression framework
* Regular performance reviews and feedback
* Opportunities for role advancement
* Cross-functional project exposure

## Knowledge Sharing
* Regular tech talks and knowledge sharing sessions
* Documentation and best practices sharing
* Collaborative learning environment
* Access to industry conferences and events

## Leadership Development
* Leadership training programs
* Project management opportunities
* Team lead roles for experienced members
* Mentoring junior team members
