# PROVIDENT FUND

The Company offers Provident funds to all its permanent team members. The
basic clauses of the policy are as follows:

**Eligibility:**

  1. All Permanent full-time working team members are eligible for the provident fund benefit.
  2. Anyone not working full-time is not eligible for this benefit.

**Employee Contribution:**

  1. Every team member who qualifies for PF will contribute a fixed amount of his salary towards the fund 
  2. The company will deduct the amount from every team member's salary every month and manage the fund.
  3. Details of PF:

Salary | PF Deduction | PF Return | Maturity Period  
---|---|---|---  
Up to 20,000/- | 500 | 1000 | 6 Months  
21K – 30K | 1000 | 2000 | 12 Months  
31K – 50K | 1500 | 3000 | 12 Months  
51K – 100K | 2500 | 5000 | 12 Months  
Above 100K | 3500 | 7000 | 12 Months  
  
**Company Contribution:**

  1. Any employee who has served for straight 12 months from his/her date of joining and falls on eligibility criteria is eligible for the company contribution to the employee's provident fund.
  2. After 12 months of the PF Deduction, the company will match the accumulated amount from the employee contribution for that month's PF. 
  3. Any Employee leaving the company before completing 12 months or multiple of 12 months will not be eligible for the company contribution for that Month.

**Deductions:**

  1. The provident fund will be subject to all tax deductions levied by the government of Pakistan.
  2. The Company reserves the right to recover any damages incurred by the employee during his service with the company from the employee's provident fund contribution.

**Payout:**

  1. The provident fund will be held with the company until the employee is working with the company.
  2. The provident fund will be released two weeks after the employee has received the last salary from the company.

**Advances:**

  1. An Employee can apply for a loan up to 50% of his accumulated provident fund.
  2. The employee is bound to repay the loan according to agreed terms and conditions
  3. The company reserves the right to recover and loan advances from the accumulated provident fund.
  4. In case of the death of an employee, the company will release its PF to the descended family within 2 weeks.
