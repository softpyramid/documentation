# EOBI

## Overview
The Employees' Old-Age Benefits Institution (EOBI) is the pension, old age benefits institution of the Government of Pakistan. All permanent employees are eligible for this policy.

## Policy Details

### Registration
Employees who are already registered with EOBI and paying contributions shall continue to do so.

### Contribution Structure
EOBI operates on a partially funded basis:
- Employer's Contribution: 5% of minimum wages (Rs. 32,000) = Rs. 1,600/= Per Month
- Employee's Contribution: 1% of minimum wages (Rs. 32,000) = Rs. 320/= Per Month
- Total contribution per Employee: Rs. 1,920/= Per Month

### Benefits
An insured person typically becomes eligible to get a pension after reaching the age of 60 and completing 15 years of insured service.

1. **Old age pension**
   - Provided in the event of retirement

2. **Survivors' pension**
   - Provided to nominees of the insured person in case of death
   - Includes old parents and young widows as per Islamic teachings

3. **Invalidity pension**
   - Provided in case of disability of the insured person

4. **Old age grant**
   - Given to those who have attained superannuation age
   - For those who don't meet minimum threshold for pension

### Pension Amount
- Minimum pension: Rs. 8,500/- (revised w.e.f August 2023)
- Maximum pension: Limited by average wages during employment and years of contribution

# EOBI

The Employees' Old-Age Benefits Institution (EOBI) is the pension, old age
benefits institution of the Government of Pakistan.

All permanent employees are eligible for this policy.

Employees who are already registered with EOBI and paying contributions shall
continue to do so.

EOBI operates on a partially funded basis:  
A contribution equal to 5% of the minimum wage has to be paid by the Employer.

Contribution equal to 1% of minimum wages by the employees.

RATES:  
Employer's Contribution @ 5% of the worker's wages (i.e. Rs. 32,000) Rs.
1600/= Per Month  
Employee's Contribution @ 1% of the worker's minimum wages (i.e Rs 32,000) Rs.
320/= Per Month.

Total contribution per Employee Rs. 1600+320 = Rs. 1920/= Per Month

Note:  
Employees' Old-Age Benefits Institution provides the following benefits to the
insured person or its survivors. An insured person typically becomes eligible
to get a pension after reaching the age of 60 and completing 15 years of
insured service.

  1. **Old age pension** – This pension is provided in the event of retirement.
  2. **Survivors' pension** – Survivors' pension is provided to the nominees of the insured person in the event of the death of the insured person. As per Islamic teachings, old parents and young widows are included in the survivor pension program.
  3. **Invalidity pension** – This pension is provided in case of disability of the insured person
  4.     1. **Old age grant** – Old age grants are given to those who have attained the superannuation age but do not meet the minimum threshold for pension

A minimum pension of ₨ 8500/- (revised w.e.f August 2023) is provided by the
scheme while a maximum pension is limited by the average wages during
employment and years of contribution to the insurance scheme.
