# **Child Birth Bonus**

Childbirth is a magical moment for every couple. Every time a child is born,
God showers unlimited blessings on the household. All permanent team members,
when distributing sweets in the office to share the good news of the
increasing population of their home, Soft Pyramid also becomes a part of the
happiness by offering up to PKR 10,000/- as a goodwill gesture to the new
parent.
