## Overtime

SOFT PYRAMID always appreciates the hard work of its employees. Every permanent
employee is eligible for an Overtime bonus. But there are some rules for this
bonus.

  1. Every employee should have prior approval for the extra hours from the CTO
  2. The rate of extra hours will double from regular pay but if your extra hour amount crosses from Rs. 2000/- then your extra hour rate will be Rs. 2000/- per hour only.
  3. Management Positions are not eligible for an Overtime Bonus.
