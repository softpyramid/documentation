# Maternity Policy

## Overview
The company provides maternity benefits to all eligible permanent resources and covers hospital expenses up to Rs. 30,000 which result in childbirth or miscarriage.

## Policy Details

### Eligibility
- Available to permanent resources after completing probation period
- Maximum of three maternities during employment with Soft Pyramid
- Excludes spouses who are employed and already entitled to similar medical facilities

### Coverage
- Hospital expenses up to Rs. 30,000 for childbirth or miscarriage
- Only medically necessary legal abortions on medical grounds are covered
- Pre and post-maternity expenses are excluded
