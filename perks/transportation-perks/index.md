## Fuel Allowance Details

### Monthly Fuel Limits
1. Admin in charge and Office Boy: 20 liters
2. Lead Positions: 25 liters
3. Managerial Positions: 30 liters
4. Higher Roles (CTO, etc.): 50 liters

### Important Notes
1. Unused fuel quota expires at the end of each month and cannot be carried forward
2. Employees serving notice period are not eligible for fuel benefits
3. Fuel limits are subject to management discretion and may be adjusted

# Fuel Perks

__

## **Fuel Perk:**

According to company Policy, Fuel Benefit is only for team-leads and higher
positions.

  1. As per Policy, 20-liter fuel is allowed to the Admin in charge, and Office Boy
  2. As per policy, 25-liter fuel is allowed for Lead Positions.
  3. As per policy, 30-liter fuel is allowed for Managerial Positions.
  4. As per policy, 50-liter fuel is allowed for Higher Roles, such as CTO, 

**Note:**

  1. If Mr. XYZ has a fuel limit of 30 liters, but he uses only 25 liters in a month. So the remaining 5 liters will expire at the last of the month and not be transferred to next month.
  2. If Mr. XYZ is in a notice period, He can't avail of his fuel benefit.
  3. The fuel Limits will be decided by the management. The upper-mentioned limit is not fixed.
