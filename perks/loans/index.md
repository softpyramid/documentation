# **SHORT-TERM PERSONAL LOAN**

* The company will review the case and the final decision is at the discretion of the management.
* All permanent employees Who served with the company for at least 1 year are eligible to apply for a loan with respect to the below-mentioned formula.

**number of years in the company / 2 * his gross salary**

Example:
The employee has spent 4 years and a gross salary of 50,000 PKR, then they would be able to apply for a loan of PKR 100,000

4 / 2 * 50,000 PKR = 100,000 PKR

* Employees will have a cap of a maximum of three salaries, for example, if an employee has spent 8 years in the company and his basic salary is 50,000 PKR, as per the formula he should be able to get 4 times his basic salary but they have a cap here which will not allow him to get a loan for more then 3 times his gross salary
* The terms, conditions & tenure for loan recovery shall be decided by the management.
* Only one loan can be approved at a time by an employee. The employee will not be eligible to apply for a second loan until his/her first loan is cleared.
* The company can demand any kind of security from the employee before the disbursement of the loan (If needed).

**Note:**  
* Anyone not working full-time is not eligible for this benefit.
* Interns do not qualify for the benefit of the personal loan.
* Team members on probation do not qualify for the benefit of the personal loan.
