# Guideline to avail Leaves

**Important Points :**

  * Leaves are Allocated on a prorated basis to all employees joining Soft Pyramid. Every employee gets every month
  * 0.5 sick leave
  * 0.5 Informed
  * 0.67 Annual.
  * For Example, Mr. XYZ joined Soft Pyramid in July 2023. He will be eligible for leave from August 2023 (If the probation period is one month). From August 2023 to December 2023, he will be allocated 1.67 leaves per month.
  * For employees on Probation, Two leaves are allowed during the whole probation period (if they avail more leave, then that will be unpaid and deducted from that month's salary).
  * Employees on probation cannot avail of WFH (work from home).
  * If you avail leave above your existing quota in any month, the leaves will be adjusted from the allocation first, and the rest will be deducted from that month's salary.
  * For Example, Mr. ABC accumulated 3 leaves from January 2022 to March 2022. He had to avail of 2 Sick leaves. Out of the 3 Sick leaves, 2 will be adjusted from his accumulated quota, and 1 will be deducted from his March 2022 salary.
  * All employees are eligible for gazetted holidays, Soft Pyramid observes holidays as per Federal Government notification.
  * No leaves accrued or accumulated are allowed during the notice period.
  * An employee is eligible for leave encashment only if he/she serves with the company until December of that particular year or at the time of final settlement.  
(Only Annual Leaves are eligible for Encashment and carry forward)

  * Let's say that till December 2023, XYZ has accumulated 8 leaves, of which he availed 4.5 leaves. This means that based on his decision, he will get a reimbursement of 3.5 leaves with December 2023 salary, or these will be carried forward to the Year 2024.
  * If an Employee takes off a day before and after Public Holidays, then the public holidays will also count as Leaves. Same for the weekend if you take leave on Friday and Monday then Saturday and Sunday will also count as leave.
