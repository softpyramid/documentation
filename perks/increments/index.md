# NEW JOINERS INCREMENT

  * Anyone who joins the company and is offered an increment after the end of the probationary period he/she will be eligible for the next increment from his date of confirmation.
  * For an employee who joins the company but does not avail an increment after probation, his/her increment period will start to form the date of joining.
