# Cellular Package

## Overview
Soft Pyramid extends a cellular postpaid package to permanent resources according to the designation in the Administrative department or above.

### Package Details

| Designation | On Net | Off-Net | SMS/CUG | 4G Data |
|-------------|---------|---------|----------|----------|
| CEO | N/A | N/A | N/A | N/A |
| CTO | N/A | N/A | N/A | N/A |
| Human Resource Department | 1000 | 300 | 1000/1500 | 1500 MB |
| Administrative Department | 400 | 100 | 400/500 | 500 MB |
