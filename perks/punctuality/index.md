## Introduction
This policy outlines the expectations for employee punctuality and attendance at SOFT PYRAMID. We value your time and commitment, and we believe in fostering a work environment that promotes efficiency and professionalism.

## Work Hours
- **Standard hours:** 10:00 am to 7:00 pm, Monday through Friday
- **Grace Period:** A 15-minute grace period is allowed after the official start time to accommodate unforeseen circumstances

## Consequences of Late Arrival
1. **Two Late Arrivals:** Documented within the same month after the grace period
2. **Third Late Arrival:**
   - A quarter-day of leave will be deducted from your leave quota
   - Opportunity for a discussion with the manager to find supportive solutions
   - Counseling services are offered for additional support

## Habitual Late Comers
- Defined as more than three late arrivals in a month or late arrivals exceeding 30 minutes on any given day
- Addressed with supportive measures and, if necessary, disciplinary actions such as verbal or written warnings
- Termination of employment is considered only after all supportive measures have been exhausted

## Positive Reinforcement
Consistent punctuality over six months will be recognized with a commendation and additional leave days as a reward.

## Management Discretion
Management reserves the right to implement additional disciplinary actions for excessive tardiness or late arrivals due to severe circumstances, ensuring fairness and consistency.

## Communication
- Employees must notify their Manager and HR in advance of any anticipated late arrival via email or phone call as early as possible
- We believe these updates will help create a more balanced and motivating environment for all our employees
- Should you have any questions or need further clarification, please feel free to reach out
