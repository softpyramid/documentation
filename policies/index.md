# SOPs

## Termination Policy

### Introduction
SOFT PYRAMID is committed to maintaining a productive, ethical, and respectful work environment for all employees. This Termination Policy outlines the principles and procedures governing the termination of employment within our organization.

### Objective
The primary objective of this policy is to provide clear guidelines for the termination of employment, ensuring that it is conducted fairly, legally, and in alignment with our company values and applicable laws.  
**Note:** The termination notice period will be the same as mentioned in the resignation SOPs.

### Types of Termination
Termination of employment at SOFT PYRAMID may occur for various reasons, including but not limited to:

#### 3.1. Voluntary Termination
When an employee chooses to resign or retire voluntarily.

#### 3.2. Involuntary Termination
When employment is terminated by the company due to performance, misconduct, or other valid reasons, as outlined below.

### Grounds for Involuntary Termination
Involuntary termination may occur for the following reasons:

#### 4.1. Poor Performance
Employees whose performance consistently falls below acceptable standards despite coaching and support may be subject to termination.

#### 4.2. Misconduct
Violation of company policies, codes of conduct, or ethical standards may result in termination. This includes but is not limited to harassment, theft, dishonesty, insubordination, and safety violations.

#### 4.3. Non-Compliance
Failure to comply with applicable laws, regulations, and licensing requirements that affect job performance and the company's reputation may lead to termination.

#### 4.4. Redundancy or Restructuring
In cases of business restructuring, workforce downsizing, or redundancy, positions may be eliminated, leading to termination.

### Termination Process

#### 5.1. Notification
Employees will receive written notice of termination through Email. The notice period may vary based on the reason for termination and local labor laws.

#### 5.2. Return of Company Property
Employees are required to return all company property, including badges, keys, laptops, and any other assets, during the notice period.

#### 5.3. Final Pay
Employees will receive their final paycheck, including any accrued but unused vacation or paid time off, in accordance with applicable labor laws.

### Exit Interviews
To gather feedback and insights, employees may be invited to participate in an exit interview. These interviews are voluntary and confidential.

### Benefits and Perks During Notice Period
Once your Termination is approved, you will not be eligible to avail of any benefits or perks during your notice period. You will only get your basic Salary at the time of clearance.
